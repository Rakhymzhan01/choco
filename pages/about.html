About choco

Your local marketplace for coupons and discounts

How it works

  1. Find a deal

    Search by category, district or price and open the offer you like.

  2. Book and pay

    Add coupons to the cart, choose the visit date and confirm the booking.

  3. Enjoy

    Show your coupon code at the venue and pay only the discounted price.

Our mission

choco helps people in Almaty discover great places and helps local businesses reach new customers. We bring together pools, spas, restaurants, clinics, beauty studios and many more in one simple website.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my coupon?

After you confirm a booking, coupon codes appear on the confirmation page and in your profile under "My bookings".

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Bookings with the status "Pending" can be cancelled from your profile.

Is this a real shop?

No. choco is a student web programming project. No real payments are processed.
